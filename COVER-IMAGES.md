# Cover images needed

One photo per post, saved as `cover.jpg` inside the post's folder (for example `content/reviews/asus-rt-be92u-review/cover.jpg`).

Rules:
- Real photographs only (Unsplash or Pexels, license allows free commercial use). No AI art, no illustrations.
- At least 1200x630 px, landscape. Hugo crops and converts it to 1200x630 webp q80 at build time, so keep the subject near the center.
- After choosing a photo, **edit `imageAlt` in the post's front matter to describe the photo you actually used**. The alt text below describes the shot we want, not a specific file.
- Optional: add `imageCredit: "Photo: [Name](url) / Unsplash"` to show a caption.
- Optional: a fallback share image at `static/images/og-default.jpg` (1200x630) is used for pages without a cover.

Until a `cover.jpg` exists, cards show a neutral placeholder tile with the topic icon.
