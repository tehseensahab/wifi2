We want to get it right. If you spot a factual error, an out-of-date price, or a broken source link, email [{{< param "contactEmail" >}}](mailto:{{< param "contactEmail" >}}) with the article link and what you found.

## How we handle corrections

- We fix factual errors as soon as we confirm them.
- For a significant change, we add a note on the article with the date and what changed.
- We update the "Last updated" date whenever we change the substance of an article.
