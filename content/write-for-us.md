WiFi Weekly publishes practical, accurate coverage of home networking for readers in the US and other English-speaking countries. We accept guest posts and sponsored posts that help those readers.

## What we publish

- **Routers and mesh Wi-Fi:** buying advice, setup and comparisons
- **Wi-Fi 6, 6E and 7:** explained clearly and accurately
- **Internet plans and providers:** how to choose, and what plans really include
- **Troubleshooting:** slow Wi-Fi, dead zones, dropouts and lag
- **Smart home and IoT connectivity**
- **Network security and privacy** at home
- **Gaming and streaming** network performance
- **Small office and work-from-home networking**

We're most interested in original experience and expertise: things you have set up, fixed, measured or know professionally.

## Our standards

Guest and sponsored posts meet the same [editorial policy]({{< relref "editorial-policy" >}}) as our own articles.

- **Original and unpublished.** No content published elsewhere, spun, or generated without human expertise and checking.
- **Accurate and sourced.** Link to primary sources for specifications and technical claims, such as manufacturer pages, standards bodies or FCC documents.
- **Honest about testing.** Say whether you physically tested a product. Never invent speed, range or latency results.
- **Useful first.** No keyword stuffing, no thin product roundups, and no articles written mainly to place links.
- **Written by a real person,** under your own name, with a short, accurate bio.

We edit every submission for accuracy, clarity and style, and we may add sources, internal links or a Fact-check notes table. We decide the final headline and publication date.

## Sponsored posts

We accept sponsored posts. Every sponsored post:

- is labeled "Sponsored" at the top of the article and names the sponsor;
- is reviewed by our editors and must meet our accuracy standards;
- uses link attributes that follow Google's guidelines for paid links (rel="sponsored").

We don't sell placement in our own reviews, rankings or recommendations, and sponsored posts never change them.

## Links

Links must help the reader. We review every link and may remove or change any that don't. Links in sponsored posts are marked as sponsored.

## What we don't accept

- Gambling, adult, cryptocurrency-trading, payday-loan or other unrelated topics
- Content that impersonates a brand or person, or makes claims you can't back up
- Fake reviews, invented test results or undisclosed paid promotion

## How to pitch

Email [editorial@wifiweekly.com](mailto:editorial@wifiweekly.com) with:

1. Your name and a one-line bio, including any networking or industry experience
2. Two or three headline ideas, each with a sentence on who it helps and why
3. Links to two published samples
4. Whether the pitch is a **guest post** or a **sponsored post**; for sponsored posts, include the brand or company involved

Please don't send a finished article before we've agreed on the topic. We reply to pitches that fit, usually within a week.
