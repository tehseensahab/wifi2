This is a short status update, based on our reporting in our [large-house router guide]({{< relref "best-wifi-router-for-large-house" >}}) and press reports as of mid-September 2026.

- **eero and Netgear** were approved in April 2026.
- **ASUS** was approved in early September.
- **TP-Link** had not been approved as of mid-September reporting.

## What it means for buyers

On March 23, 2026, the FCC added consumer routers produced in foreign countries to its Covered List, which blocks new models from receiving FCC authorization. Models already authorized can still be sold, and routers you own are unaffected. The FCC has waived its firmware-update cutoff for existing models until January 1, 2029.

If you're buying a mesh system, our [mesh guide for large houses]({{< relref "best-mesh-wifi-for-large-house" >}}) notes which models are affected. We'll update this post when any company's status changes.
