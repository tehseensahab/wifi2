The ASUS RT-BE92U is a tri-band Wi-Fi 7 router with a 10G port, aimed at homes with multi-gig internet and newer devices. This is a research-based overview built from ASUS's product pages and a US retailer's specification listing. WiFi Weekly has not physically tested the RT-BE92U, so there is no score and there are no speed or latency measurements here.

*Correction, November 14, 2026: an earlier version of this page presented test results, a score and a price that were not backed by testing records or a dated source. They have been removed.*

## What is the ASUS RT-BE92U?

| Specification | What is listed | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Wi-Fi | Tri-band Wi-Fi 7, BE9700 class | ASUS |
| Maximum link rates | 2.4 GHz 1032 Mbps, 5 GHz 2882 Mbps, 6 GHz 5764 Mbps | B&H listing |
| Wi-Fi 7 features | 320 MHz channels, 4096-QAM, Multi-Link Operation (MLO) | ASUS |
| Ports | One 10G WAN/LAN port and four 2.5G Ethernet ports | ASUS; B&H lists one of the 2.5G ports as WAN/LAN |
| Wired capacity | Up to 20G in total | ASUS |
| Mesh | AiMesh, with wireless backhaul or wired 10G or 2.5G backhaul | ASUS |
| Security | ASUS Network Security, described as subscription-free | ASUS |

The BE9700 figure is the sum of the three bands' maximum link rates. No single device connects at that speed, and none of it changes what your internet plan delivers. ASUS's US specification page did not come back in our search, so the ASUS details above are from its Ireland and UK pages; hardware can differ by region.

## Who does the RT-BE92U suit?

| Your situation | Does it fit? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Internet plan above 1 Gbps | Yes | The 10G and 2.5G ports can carry a multi-gig plan to wired devices |
| Wi-Fi 6E or Wi-Fi 7 phones and laptops | Yes | They can use the 6 GHz band |
| Plan under 1 Gbps, mostly older devices | Probably more than you need | The ports and 6 GHz band go unused |
| Large or multi-floor home | Maybe, with nodes | One router has one location; see [Wi-Fi router vs. mesh]({{< relref "/guides/wifi-router-vs-mesh" >}}) |

If MLO is the feature you are buying for, read [Wi-Fi 7 MLO explained]({{< relref "/guides/wifi-7-mlo" >}}) first: ASUS itself notes that supported MLO modes and bands vary by device. To decide whether you need multi-gig ports at all, see [do you need a multi-gig router]({{< relref "/guides/do-you-need-a-multi-gig-router" >}}).

## What we could not verify

- Real-world speed, range and latency. We have no test records and cite no independent benchmark for this model yet.
- A current US price. We did not open a US retailer's price on the day of this update, so we don't quote one.
- How long ASUS will supply firmware updates. Our guide to [checking whether a router still gets security updates]({{< relref "/guides/is-my-router-still-getting-security-updates" >}}) shows how to look this up.

## Bottom line

On its documented specifications, the RT-BE92U fits a home with a multi-gig plan and 6 GHz-capable devices. Treat that as a starting point and look for an independent test before you buy.
