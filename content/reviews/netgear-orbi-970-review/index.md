The Orbi 970 is the first mesh kit we've tested that didn't visibly slow down under a 20-device stress test, including two simultaneous 4K uploads.

The catch: advanced threat protection and parental controls sit behind a $99/year subscription after the first year. Basic firewall and guest network features stay free, which is the right call, but it's worth knowing before you buy.
