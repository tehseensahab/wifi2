The NETGEAR Orbi 970 is a quad-band Wi-Fi 7 mesh system with 10 Gig ports, built for very large homes on multi-gig internet. This is a research-based overview built from NETGEAR's product pages. WiFi Weekly has not physically tested the Orbi 970, so there is no score and there are no speed or stress-test results here.

*Correction, November 14, 2026: an earlier version of this page presented test results, a score, prices and a coverage figure that were not backed by testing records or a dated source. They have been removed or corrected.*

## What is the Orbi 970?

| Specification | What NETGEAR lists |
|---|---|
| Wi-Fi | Quad-band Wi-Fi 7 with a dedicated backhaul band |
| Class | BE27000: 11,530 + 8,647 + 5,765 + 1,147 Mbps maximum link rates |
| 6 GHz radio | 4x4, 320 MHz channels, 4K-QAM |
| Coverage | Up to 3,300 sq ft per unit; up to 10,000 sq ft for one router and two satellites |
| Devices | Up to 200 |
| Router ports | One 10 Gig internet port, one 10 Gbps LAN port, four 2.5 Gbps LAN ports |
| Satellite ports | One 10 Gbps LAN port, two 2.5 Gbps LAN ports |
| Security | One year of NETGEAR Armor included, then a paid subscription |

BE27000 is the sum of four radios' maximum link rates, which NETGEAR notes are derived from the IEEE 802.11 specification. It is not a speed any one device reaches. Coverage figures are NETGEAR's own.

## What we could not verify

- Real-world speed, range and behavior with many devices. We have no test records for this system.
- A current US price for the system or for NETGEAR Armor. We did not open a dated US price page for this update, so we don't quote one.

## Bottom line

On its documented specifications, the Orbi 970 is the top of NETGEAR's Orbi range and fits very large homes on multi-gig plans. Most homes will be served by a smaller system, so compare before spending at this level.
