eero's own store listed the eero Pro 6E 2-pack at $259.99 on November 14, 2026, against an original price of $349.99. That is a $90 cut on eero's own figures. The page carried an "Early Black Friday" banner and gave no end date, so check the price before you buy.

*Correction, November 14, 2026: an earlier version of this page reported a $189 price and said it was the lowest we had tracked. We could not verify either claim, so both have been removed. Prices below were read from eero.com on the date shown. We have not tested the eero Pro 6E.*

## What do you get with the eero Pro 6E?

| Specification | What eero lists |
|---|---|
| Wi-Fi | Wi-Fi 6E, with access to the 6 GHz band |
| Speed | Up to 2.3 Gbps in total: up to a gigabit wired plus up to 1.6 Gbps wireless |
| Ports per unit | One 2.5 GbE and one gigabit Ethernet port, both auto-sensing |
| Coverage (2-pack) | Up to 4,000 sq ft |
| Devices | 100+ |
| Warranty | 1 year |

The speed and coverage figures are eero's own maximums. Walls, floors and your internet plan set what you actually get.

## Who should buy it, and who should pick Wi-Fi 7?

| Your situation | Reasonable choice | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Plan around 1 Gbps or slower, phones and laptops with Wi-Fi 6E | eero Pro 6E at this price | You can use its 6 GHz band, and eero's stated wired limit is a gigabit |
| Multi-gig plan | An eero 7 model | Compare port speeds first in our [eero 7 vs. eero Pro 7 vs. eero Max 7]({{< relref "/guides/eero-7-vs-eero-pro-7-vs-eero-max-7" >}}) guide |
| New Wi-Fi 7 phones or laptops | A Wi-Fi 7 system | See [Wi-Fi 6E vs. Wi-Fi 7]({{< relref "/guides/wifi-6e-vs-wifi-7" >}}) for what you gain |
| No 6 GHz devices at all | A cheaper Wi-Fi 6 kit may do | See [Wi-Fi 6 vs. Wi-Fi 6E]({{< relref "/guides/wifi-6-vs-wifi-6e" >}}) |

eero says the Pro 6E is backward-compatible with earlier eero devices, so it can also extend an existing eero network. Before you pay for eero Plus on top, read [is eero Plus worth it]({{< relref "/guides/is-eero-plus-worth-it" >}}).

## Bottom line

At $259.99 from eero's own store, the Pro 6E 2-pack is a fair buy for a home with Wi-Fi 6E devices and a plan of about 1 Gbps or less. If you are on a multi-gig plan or buying Wi-Fi 7 devices this year, put the money toward an eero 7 model.
