We checked the price history: this is the lowest the Eero Pro 6E 2-pack has been since launch, not an inflated "was" price. At $189 it undercuts the Wi-Fi 7 version by nearly $140.

Buy this if your internet plan is under 1Gbps and your devices are a mix of phones and laptops from the last four years. Skip it and save for the Wi-Fi 7 model if you already have multi-gig service or newer 7-capable devices.
