Asus's RT-BE92U is the fastest router we've tested this year on a real home network, not just a lab bench. Over a 15-device household with two 4K streams, a cloud backup job, and a kid mid-raid on a console, median latency stayed under 12ms.

## The good

Throughput on the 6GHz band held above 1.4Gbps at 20 feet through a single drywall wall, which is a genuine jump over last year's RT-BE88U. Band steering also worked without manual nudging, something we couldn't say about the previous model.

## The annoying part

The AsusWRT mobile app still buries the QoS controls three menus deep, and firmware updates require a manual check rather than a push notification. If you're coming from a mesh system with a simpler app, budget twenty minutes to find your footing.

## Verdict

If you have a gigabit-plus connection and devices that actually support Wi-Fi 7, this is the router to beat. If you're still on a 300Mbps plan, save the money and look at last year's model instead.
