This is a plain-English summary of how WiFi Weekly handles information. Have it reviewed by a lawyer before you rely on it as your legal policy.

## What we collect

- **Newsletter.** If you subscribe, we collect your email address so we can send the newsletter. You can unsubscribe from any email.
- **Server logs.** Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, processes standard request data (such as IP address and browser type) to deliver and secure the site.
- **Contact.** If you email us, we keep your message and address so we can reply.

## Affiliate links

When you click an affiliate link, the retailer may set cookies to credit us for a sale. Their privacy policies apply on their sites.

## Your choices

You can unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time, or email [{{< param "contactEmail" >}}](mailto:{{< param "contactEmail" >}}) to ask us to delete your information.
