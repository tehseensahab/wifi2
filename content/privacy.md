This policy explains what information WiFi Weekly (wifiweekly.com) collects when you visit, how it is used, and what you can do about it.

## What we collect

- **Newsletter.** If you subscribe, we collect your email address so we can send the newsletter. You can unsubscribe from any email.
- **Contact.** If you email us, we keep your message and email address so we can reply.
- **Server logs.** Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, processes standard request data (such as IP address and browser type) to deliver and secure the site.
- **Analytics.** We use Google Analytics to understand which pages are read and how visitors find the site. It uses cookies or similar identifiers and collects information such as pages viewed, device and browser type, and approximate location.

## Advertising and cookies

WiFi Weekly uses Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site or other websites.

- Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and other sites on the internet.
- You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com), or opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at [aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info).
- You can learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services at [policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).
- You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Some parts of the site may work less well without cookies.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, ads and measurement that use cookies are shown according to your consent choices where a consent message is displayed.

## Affiliate links

When you click an affiliate link, the retailer may set cookies to credit us for a sale. Their privacy policies apply on their sites. See our [affiliate disclosure]({{< relref "affiliate-disclosure" >}}).

## Third-party links

Our articles link to other websites. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

## Children

WiFi Weekly is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.
