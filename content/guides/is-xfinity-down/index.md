To find out if Xfinity is down, use one of Xfinity's three official checks: the Xfinity Outage Map at xfinity.com/support/statusmap, the Xfinity app, or a text of OUT to 266278. If none of them shows an outage at your address, report one from the map and then restart your gateway. Our tip: if your home Wi-Fi is down, run these checks from your phone with Wi-Fi turned off so it uses cellular data.

The steps below come from Xfinity's own support pages, last updated between August and October 2026. Third-party "is it down" sites collect user reports, which can tell you others are having trouble, but Xfinity's own tools are the ones that show an outage at your address with an estimated time to fix.

## How to check if Xfinity is down

| Method | How | Sign-in needed? | What it tells you |
|---|---|---|---|
| Outage Map | Go to xfinity.com/support/statusmap, or open Xfinity Support, scroll down and click View Outage Map | No: "You can search for any address serviced by Xfinity" | Outage description, type and cause, estimated time to fix, repair status |
| Xfinity app | Sign in; if your location is affected, outage information shows. Tap View outage details | Yes | Description, type and cause, estimated repair time |
| Text message | Text OUT to 266278 (COMCST) | No | Whether there is an outage in your area. Message and data rates may apply |

### Check the Xfinity Outage Map

1. Go to **xfinity.com/support/statusmap**, or open the Xfinity Support page, scroll down and click **View Outage Map**.
2. If you're signed in, click the **house icon** to see your own location. If not, enter the address.
3. If there is an outage, the map shows a banner, a short description with next steps, the type and cause, and "The estimated time to fix the outage and status of repairs (if applicable)."

Xfinity doesn't say how accurate its time-to-fix estimates are, so treat them as a guide.

### Check in the Xfinity app

Xfinity says: "Sign in to the Xfinity app and if your location is impacted, outage information shows." Tap **View outage details** for the description, outage type and cause, and repair status.

### Text OUT to 266278

Xfinity says to "text OUT to 266278 (COMCST) to check for outages in your area." This is the quickest option when your phone has a signal but you don't want to open a browser or app.

## How to get a text when Xfinity service is back

If your mobile number is registered for Xfinity texts, you may get texts for certain outages, with "an estimated time to fix, periodic updates, and a final message when the services are fixed." To sign up for updates on a specific outage:

1. Open the Outage Map and sign in.
2. Click **Request Text Updates** in the outage banner or the Home icon.
3. Enter your mobile number and select **Get a Text**. You'll get a confirmation message.

## What to do if Xfinity shows no outage

### 1. Report the problem

On the Outage Map, Xfinity says: "If you're experiencing service issues and an outage isn't reported, click the **Report an outage** link."

### 2. Restart your gateway or modem

Xfinity's restart instructions:

- **In the Xfinity app (rented gateways only):** tap the **WiFi** icon, tap **Troubleshoot**, then select **Restart your Gateway**.
- **By hand (Xfinity's final option):** unplug the power cable, wait one minute, and plug it back in.

"The restart process takes up to 12 minutes. During the restart, you won't be able to use your home network." If you have Xfinity Voice, Xfinity warns that "you won't be able to make or receive calls, including emergency 911 calls," from your home phone until the gateway is back online.

Restart, don't reset: a factory reset is a different step that wipes your settings (see our [router reset guide](/guides/reset-router/)), and it isn't part of Xfinity's outage steps.

### 3. Read the gateway's lights

The light on an Xfinity gateway tells you whether the problem is the connection from Xfinity or your Wi-Fi at home. Our guide to [Xfinity gateway lights](/guides/xfinity-gateway-lights/) explains each color. If only some devices drop off while the gateway shows a good connection, see [why Wi-Fi keeps disconnecting](/guides/wifi-keeps-disconnecting/).

### 4. Check whether the power is out

If your home has lost power, your gateway is off too unless it is on battery backup, and Xfinity equipment in your neighborhood may also be affected. Our guide to [whether internet works in a power outage](/guides/internet-power-outage/) covers battery backup options, including Xfinity's own backup option.

## Can you get a credit for an Xfinity outage?

Possibly. Xfinity's credits page lists "Service interruptions or outages" under "Common reasons for credits." It does not say outage credits are applied automatically, set a minimum outage length, or promise an amount. It says you can "ask for a credit from the Xfinity Assistant." Note the date and length of the outage, then ask once service is back.
