NETGEAR Armor is worth it if you'll actually install its Bitdefender security app on your household's phones and computers and you value the extended warranty and priority support that come with it. At $99.99 a year in the US (NETGEAR's price on November 13, 2026), it's a security-software bundle, not a Wi-Fi upgrade. Armor Plus, at $149.99 a year, is only worth the extra $50 if you'd use its unlimited VPN and ad blocking. If you just want a fast, secure Orbi or Nighthawk network, let the free trial lapse: you won't be charged, and the router keeps its own security features.

This guide is based on NETGEAR's purchase page, product page and help articles, plus NETGEAR's January 2025 announcement of the current plans. We haven't tested Armor's malware blocking ourselves.

## What NETGEAR Armor costs

| Plan | US price (checked Nov. 13, 2026) | Devices | What it adds |
|---|---|---|---|
| NETGEAR Armor | $99.99/year ($8.33/month, billed annually) | 50 | Router-level protection, Bitdefender app, extended warranty, priority support |
| NETGEAR Armor Plus | $149.99/year ($12.50/month, billed annually) | 50 | Everything in Armor, plus unlimited VPN, ad blocking and anti-tracking |

Prices are from [NETGEAR's purchase page](https://www.netgear.com/home/services/armor/buy-armor/). Both plans are billed annually; NETGEAR doesn't list a month-to-month option there. NETGEAR's [pricing help article](https://kb.netgear.com/000059444/What-is-the-yearly-subscription-price-for-NETGEAR-Armor) adds that "the 1-year (annual) subscription price for Armor depends on the region in which you live," and suggests checking the Orbi or Nighthawk app, where you buy and manage the subscription.

Over three years, Armor adds $299.97 and Armor Plus $449.97 to the cost of your router at those annual rates.

## What you get

NETGEAR says Armor "works at two levels."

**On your home network.** Armor is built into Orbi and Nighthawk routers and, in NETGEAR's words, "automatically helps protect your WiFi connected devices from malware, ransomware, and phishing." That should cover devices you can't install security software on, such as smart TVs. NETGEAR's [feature list](https://kb.netgear.com/000059435/What-are-NETGEAR-Armor-features) (last updated September 9, 2026) includes advanced malware protection, a network vulnerability assessment, anomaly detection, brute-force and DDoS protection, an intrusion detection system and dark web monitoring, which alerts you when your personal information turns up in data breaches. The same article notes that "a small number of older NETGEAR devices do not support all of the Armor features listed here."

**On your devices, anywhere.** You can "install the NETGEAR Armor app on up to 50 Windows, macOS, Android, and iOS devices." NETGEAR says the app "extends antivirus, scam protection, and privacy tools to your phones, laptops, and tablets when you're away from home." Armor is powered by Bitdefender.

**Warranty and support.** Since January 2025, Armor has included an extended warranty and priority support. NETGEAR's announcement says "the extended warranty covers repairs, accidental damage coverage and free express replacement service," with a footnote that "restrictions apply," and doesn't state how long the extension lasts. Priority support "gives subscribers access to NETGEAR experts around the clock via chat, email and phone."

**What Armor Plus adds.** NETGEAR says Armor Plus "includes all the benefits of Armor and adds unlimited VPN for up to 50 devices," along with ad blocking and anti-tracking. The base plan's app has a VPN feature too, but NETGEAR's announcement doesn't spell out its limits; [Dong Knows Tech's breakdown](https://dongknows.com/netgear-armor-get-a-new-plus-service-tier/) lists unlimited VPN, ad blocking and anti-tracking as Plus-only.

## Which routers support it

NETGEAR's [Armor page](https://netgear.com/home/services/armor/) says Armor "is available on most Nighthawk routers and Orbi mesh systems," and names the Nighthawk RS700S, RS600, RS500 and RS300 and the Orbi 970, 870, 770 and 370 series as examples. If you're choosing between those Orbi systems, our [Orbi 770 vs. 870 vs. 970 comparison](/guides/orbi-770-vs-870-vs-970/) covers the hardware.

## Who should pay for it

**Pay for Armor if** you'd otherwise buy antivirus for several family devices anyway, you like having one app and one bill for it, and the extended warranty and priority support matter to you on an expensive Orbi system. Using the device app is what turns Armor from a router feature into something you'll notice.

**Pay for Armor Plus if** you'd also use a VPN on public Wi-Fi and want ad blocking, and you'd otherwise buy a separate VPN. The step up is $50 a year.

**Skip it if** you already pay for antivirus and a VPN, or you only want your Wi-Fi to work. NETGEAR's Armor page says its routers already include protections such as WPA3 encryption and automatic firmware updates, and describes Armor as "an extra layer of protection on top." Free steps such as the ones in our [router hardening checklist](/guides/router-hardening-checklist/) cover the basics without a subscription.

**Need parental controls?** Armor isn't a parental-controls plan. NETGEAR sells Smart Parental Controls separately; our [parental controls guide](/guides/best-router-for-parental-controls/) compares it with other brands.

## Armor vs. eero Plus

If you're still choosing a mesh system, the subscriptions cost about the same at the base tier. We found eero Plus at $9.99 a month or $99.99 a year in the US on October 31, 2026, matching Armor's $99.99 annual price, and eero also offers monthly billing. The bundles differ: eero Plus pairs network filters and ad blocking with partner apps (a VPN, 1Password and Malwarebytes), while Armor centers on Bitdefender security plus a warranty extension and support. Our [eero Plus guide](/guides/is-eero-plus-worth-it/) and [eero vs. Orbi comparison](/guides/eero-vs-orbi/) go further.

## Trial, refunds and canceling

- **Free trial:** NETGEAR's pricing help article says you can try Armor free for 30 days. Its cancellation article says, "If you have a free trial of a NETGEAR service, you will not be charged when it ends."
- **Refunds:** "If you cancel your NETGEAR service subscription within 30 days of the purchase date, you can receive a refund upon request." It isn't automatic: for Armor, you fill out NETGEAR's Armor Cancellation/Refund Form. "Subscriptions purchased more than 30 days ago are not eligible for a refund."
- **Canceling:** in the Orbi or Nighthawk app, go to Menu > MY SUBSCRIPTIONS, tap the service and turn off Auto renew. You can also cancel at my.netgear.com, or in your Bitdefender account if you bought Armor there. After you cancel, "your service stays active for the remaining duration of your subscription."
- **After a factory reset:** NETGEAR says you need to "manually reactivate Armor or Smart Parental Controls (SPC) services after performing a factory reset." Our [reset guide](/guides/reset-router/) covers the steps.
