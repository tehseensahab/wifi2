**On an Xfinity Gateway, solid white means it's working, solid red means it has no internet connection, and slow-blinking blue means it's pairing a device over WPS.** White that blinks during setup means activation isn't finished, and Xfinity says any other color combination means the gateway is still connecting to its systems, so wait about 15 minutes before you troubleshoot. Those meanings come from Xfinity's [gateway lights page](https://www.xfinity.com/support/articles/xfi-gateway-led-lights), last updated August 31, 2026.

This guide is based on Xfinity's support pages; we haven't tested the gateways ourselves. Xfinity's lights page doesn't split its chart by model, so the meanings below apply to its gateways in general. If you have an Xfinity Pro WiFi Extender, its lights are covered in our [Pro WiFi Extender lights guide](/guides/xfinity-pro-wifi-extender-lights/).

## Xfinity Gateway light chart

| Light | What Xfinity says it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Off | "Off" | Check power; see below |
| Solid white (or white with a purple tint), after activation | "On or operational" | Nothing |
| White, solid or blinking, during activation | "Limited operational" | Finish activation using your Getting Started guide |
| Solid red | "No internet connection" | Check for an outage, restart, then contact Xfinity |
| Blinking blue | "WPS mode" | Finish pairing the device, or wait |
| Any other color combination | "Connecting to Xfinity systems" | Wait about 15 minutes |

Source: [Xfinity](https://www.xfinity.com/support/articles/xfi-gateway-led-lights).

Not sure which gateway you have? Xfinity says the model number is on "the equipment's bottom panel," and you can also find it on your Devices page on xfinity.com or in the Xfinity app. Its [gateway overview](https://www.xfinity.com/support/articles/about-the-wireless-gateway) maps model numbers to names; for example, the XB8 is the CGM4981COM. Our [XB10 vs. XB8 guide](/guides/xfinity-xb10-vs-xb8/) compares the XB10 and XB8.

## Solid white: working normally

Solid white after activation is the normal state. Xfinity notes it may have a purple tint. If the light is white but your devices are slow or dropping off, Xfinity treats white as operational, so look next at Wi-Fi signal or the device itself; our guide to [slow Wi-Fi on a fast connection](/guides/wifi-slow-but-internet-fast/) covers that.

During activation, white can be solid or blinking, and Xfinity calls that "limited operational." Its advice: "If you haven't yet activated your device, follow the steps outlined in your Getting Started guide." Xfinity's lights page also recommends the Xfinity app for activation.

## Solid red: no internet connection

Xfinity lists solid red as "No internet connection" and says "We're having trouble connecting with your device." Its lights page tells you to contact Xfinity, but two checks first can save a call:

1. **Check for an outage.** Xfinity's [outage page](https://www.xfinity.com/support/articles/check-service-outage) lists three ways: sign in to the Xfinity app ("if your location is impacted, outage information shows"), use the Xfinity Outage Map at xfinity.com/support/statusmap, or "text OUT to 266278 (COMCST)." Message and data rates may apply. If your home internet is down, use phone data or another connection for these checks.
2. **Restart the gateway** (steps below). If the light stays red after the restart finishes, contact Xfinity.

## Blinking blue: WPS pairing mode

Blinking blue means "WPS mode": the gateway "is trying to make a connection with another wireless device," according to Xfinity. Xfinity's WPS page says this happens when you press the gateway's WPS button.

Xfinity's [WPS page](https://www.xfinity.com/support/articles/wifi-protected-setup) says that on XB6, XB7 and XB8 gateways "the blue LED light will slowly blink, indicating it's in the WPS pairing mode," and that it turns white after pairing. To finish pairing, use WPS on the printer, extender or other device. Xfinity's steps say: "Press the WPS button on your gateway within two minutes." On the XB7 and XB8, the page's photo captions show the WPS button in the middle of the gateway.

Once a device connects, Xfinity says, "your new device should be up and running." The Xfinity pages we checked don't say how to cancel WPS if you started it by accident. Our guide on [whether to turn off WPS](/guides/should-you-disable-wps-and-upnp/) explains the security trade-off.

## Blinking amber, orange or any other color

Xfinity's main lights page doesn't list amber or orange by name. It covers "all other color combinations" with one meaning, "Connecting to Xfinity systems," and one instruction: "Wait for about 15 minutes for this cycle to complete." If you're still having trouble after that, it says to contact Xfinity.

Xfinity does mention amber elsewhere. On its [Pro WiFi Extender lights page](https://www.xfinity.com/support/articles/storm-ready-lights), last updated October 19, 2026, the gateway shows "Blink amber" during a "Network outage." That chart covers homes with the Pro WiFi Extender, but it's a good reason to check for an outage if the amber blinking goes on for more than 15 minutes.

## Light off

Xfinity's chart simply lists no light as "Off." If your internet is down and the gateway is dark, check that its power cable is connected at both ends and that the outlet works. If you have an Xfinity Pro WiFi Extender, Xfinity's extender chart shows the gateway off during a power outage; our guide to [internet during a power outage](/guides/internet-power-outage/) explains what keeps working.

## How to restart an Xfinity Gateway

Xfinity's [restart page](https://www.xfinity.com/support/internet/power-cycling-your-modem/) recommends running its full network test first, then restarting:

**In the Xfinity app**, for gateways rented from Xfinity:

1. "Locate the **WiFi** icon."
2. "Tap the **Troubleshoot** option."
3. "Select **Restart your Gateway**."

**Manually**, which Xfinity treats as a last resort:

1. "Unplug the power cable."
2. Wait one minute.
3. "Plug the power cable back in."

Xfinity says the restart "takes up to 12 minutes," so give it the full time before judging the light. A restart isn't a factory reset; our [reboot vs. factory reset guide](/guides/reset-router/) explains the difference.

## When to contact Xfinity

Contact Xfinity if the light stays solid red after a full restart and there's no outage, or if it keeps cycling through other colors well past 15 minutes. Xfinity's lights page also points to its online troubleshooter and support center.
