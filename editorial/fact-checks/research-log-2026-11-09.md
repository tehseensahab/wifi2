## B. Phone as backup internet (guides/use-phone-as-backup-internet)

Source B1: https://asus.com/support/faq/1045247 (ASUS, Last Update 2024/07/11)
- "Once setup is applied, Dual WAN is automatically enabled."
- Android steps: Network Map > Android icon > GO; turn USB mode on, choose Android smartphone, Apply. Firmware lists: 3.0.0.4.388.23000-29999, beyond 3.0.0.4.388.31900, 3.0.0.4.386.xxxxx beyond 69019 (newer firmware handled by FAQ 1050074).
Source B2: https://www.asus.com/us/support/FAQ/1050074 (ASUS US, Last Update 2026/03/03)
- Title "Plug, Share & Surf: Turn Your ASUS Router into a 4G/5G Hotspot Instantly (4G / 5G Auto Mobile Tethering)"
- "This feature is available for specific 802.11ax models with certain firmware versions:" (same three firmware ranges)
- iPhone: turn on Personal Hotspot, plug into router USB port; "Tap “Trust” if you receive an alert that says Trust this computer?"
- Android: connect to USB port, turn on USB tethering.
- "Upon the availability of your backup WAN, you shall see the hotspot icon as below."
- "For help with cellular network services, contact your Phone’s Internet Service Provider (ISP)."
Source B3: https://www.asus.com/support/FAQ/1011719 (ASUS Dual WAN, Last Update 2025/08/13)
- "Select Failover mode to use a secondary WAN for backup network access."
- "will revert to the Primary WAN if failback has been enabled." (Allow Failback Function)
- "Go to WAN > Dual WAN > Switch the button on [Enable Dual WAN] to ON (Default is OFF)"
- "the system will automatically detect your network status by periodically sending DNS queries"
- "You cannot select the same WAN type at the same time."
Source B4: https://docs.gl-inet.com/router/en/4/interface_guide/internet_tethering/ (GL.iNet firmware 4 docs, undated)
- "Using a USB cable to share network from your smartphone to the router is called Tethering."
- iPhone: Settings > Personal Hotspot > "Allow Others to Join"; admin panel INTERNET > Tethering > Connect; "A system dialog will appear asking whether to trust the device."
- "Some mobile carriers limit or charge extra for tethering."
- Troubleshooting: use a USB cable that supports data transfer (fetch summary)
Source B5: https://docs.gl-inet.com/router/en/4/interface_guide/multi-wan/
- "There are two modes for Multi-WAN: Failover and Load Balance."
- Fails over to "the next available interface with the highest priority"; switches back when higher-priority link returns (summary)
- Multi-WAN interface list includes "Tethering"; detection by ping to track IP (summary)
- "Switching to high sensitivity may lead to network disconnection, please adjust it with caution."
Source B6: https://eero.com/support/articles/how-do-i-set-up-and-manage-eero-hotspot-backup (eero, undated)
- "an eero Plus (formerly eero Secure+) subscription, or qualifying ISP or Ring subscription, all sold separately."
- "eero App updated to version 6.25.1 or later"; eeroOS "updated to 6.13.0 or later"
- "USB and Ethernet based backup internet sources are not supported."
- "eero does not guarantee zero disruption and brief network downtime may occur during failover."
- "Customer is responsible for applicable data charges with backup internet connection providers and ensuring authorized use."
- Not supported on bridge mode networks or TKIP WiFi networks.
Source B7: https://support.google.com/android/answer/9059108?hl=en (Google Android Help)
- USB: "Connect your phone to the other device with a USB cable." ... "Touch and hold Hotspot" ... "Turn on USB tethering."
- "Some mobile carriers limit or charge extra for tethering. We recommend checking with your carrier."
- "Plug in your devices while tethering."
Source B8: https://support.apple.com/kb/ht4517 (Apple, Published Date: May 07, 2026)
- Personal Hotspot "requires a wireless carrier that supports Personal Hotspot"; extra fees may apply; device count depends on carrier and model (summary)
- "if asked to trust this computer, tap Trust."
