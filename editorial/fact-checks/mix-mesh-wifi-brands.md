"we don't recommend this setup"; bridging -> "You'll lose access to advanced networking features in the eero app."; "we recommend bridging your existing router instead of your eeros." | https://eero.com/support/articles/does-eero-replace-my-current-router | undated
- Deco mixing | "TP-Link Deco systems are designed to work across models, so you can combine different Deco units in the same mesh network."; "the overall network performance will be influenced by the capabilities of the slower units in the system."; main Deco determines app features; X90 example swaps Antivirus, Parental Control, QoS for HomeShield | https://www.tp-link.com/us/faq-2248.html | Last updated November 3, 2026 | US
- X50-Outdoor | "Deco X50-Outdoor cannot wirelessly relay WiFi signals from other routers or APs."; wired to LAN port "you can configure as Main Deco for use (both router mode or access point mode are fine)" | https://www.omadanetworks.com/us/support/faq/3611/ | Feb 26, 2024 | US
