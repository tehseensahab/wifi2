## Article B: Is Xfinity Down? (content/guides/is-xfinity-down/index.md)

X1 https://www.xfinity.com/support/articles/check-service-outage (published 2025-06-24, modified 2026-08-31 in metadata). X2 /check-xfinity-outage-map and X3 /report-issues-xfinity-services and /support/internet/view-outages-apple-android-devices/ return the same page/metadata.
- "Sign in to the Xfinity app and if your location is impacted, outage information shows." Tap "View outage details" (description, outage type and cause, repair status).
- Outage Map: https://www.xfinity.com/support/statusmap, QR code, or Support page → scroll → "View Outage Map." Signed-in users click house icon; others enter an address. "You can search for any address serviced by Xfinity."
- "text OUT to 266278 (COMCST) to check for outages in your area." data and messaging rates may apply.
- Registered numbers may get texts for certain outages with "an estimated time to fix, periodic updates, and a final message when the services are fixed."
- Request Text Updates: sign in on Outage Map, click "Request Text Updates" in banner or Home icon, enter mobile number, select "Get a Text"; confirmation message.
- Map shows "The estimated time to fix the outage and status of repairs (if applicable)." Map banner "Internet outage detected".
- "If you're experiencing service issues and an outage isn't reported, click the Report an outage link."

X4 https://www.xfinity.com/support/internet/power-cycling-your-modem/ (published 2026-06-26, modified 2026-08-31)
- "If you rent a gateway, you can use the Xfinity app to restart the gateway." "Locate the WiFi icon," "Tap the Troubleshoot option," "Select Restart your Gateway."
- final attempt: "Unplug the power cable." "Wait one minute." "Plug the power cable back in."
- "The restart process takes up to 12 minutes. During the restart, you won't be able to use your home network."
- "If you have Xfinity Voice, you won't be able to make or receive calls, including emergency 911 calls," "from your home phone until your gateway is back online."

X5 https://www.xfinity.com/support/articles/understand-credits-refunds (published/modified 2026-10-16)
- "Common reasons for credits": "Service interruptions or outages." Page doesn't say automatic, no minimum. "ask for a credit from the Xfinity Assistant".
