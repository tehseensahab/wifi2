## Independent fact-check (2026-10-28, separate agent)

Re-fetched: eero store (price confirmed $169.99 / $284.99, no sale shown), NETGEAR RS300 (all specs and footnotes confirmed).

- VERIFIED: all BE230, RT-BE58U, eero 7 and RS300 specs, ports, coverage, device counts, footnotes; 688 + 2,882 = 3,570 Mbps (~3.6 Gbps); eero price; TP-Link lawsuit facts (internal post); 6 GHz device requirement (internal band guide).
- NOT VERIFIED, fixed: "budget"/"lowest-cost"/"most for the least" (no prices confirmed for BE230/RT-BE58U) → reworded to "entry-level BE3600 class"; "manufacturer claims for open conditions" → "manufacturer claims".
- QUALIFIED, fixed: NETGEAR "limited to indoor range" is an indoor-use limit, not a range statement → quote removed, range claim attributed to band guide; eero 7 "radios"/"smart-home hub" → "supports Thread and Zigbee, Matter controller, may replace a hub for compatible devices"; ASUS vs eero comparisons reframed as interface facts (web UI vs app-only); "6 GHz router gives them nothing" → "the 6 GHz band gives them nothing"; 2.5 Gbps "port" → "internet port"; added "TP-Link denies the claims"; ASUS row marked "Verified (Canadian page)".
- Unsourced general advice kept as advice (building internet FAQ, firmware updates).
