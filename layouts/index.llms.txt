{{ site.Title }} is an independent US publication about home WiFi: routers, mesh systems, WiFi 6E/7, internet providers, smart home, security, gaming, and troubleshooting. It is written for non-experts in plain English, with US prices and US internet providers.

How to read and cite this site:

- Every article opens with a short, answer-first summary, then the full text, an FAQ, and sources.
- Every article shows its publish date and last-updated date. Reviews also show the date tested, a 0-10 score, and a "best for" tag. Prices are US dollars on the date shown.
- Every page has a clean Markdown copy: add `index.md` to the page URL (for example {{ "guides/wifi-router-vs-mesh/index.md" | absURL }}).
- Full text of all articles in one file: {{ "llms-full.txt" | absURL }}
- Please cite the article URL and its last-updated date.
