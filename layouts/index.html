How we test

  1. 1

    Labeled honestly

    Each product article says whether it was physically tested, built from specs and outside test data, or is an explainer. We never call something tested when it was not.

  2. 2

    Dated and updated

    Articles show when they were published and updated. Tested reviews also show the test date and firmware.

  3. 3

    Sources linked

    Specs, FCC filings, and outside data are linked at the bottom of each article so you can check us.

  4. 4

    Affiliate disclosure

    Some links earn us a commission. That never changes a score or a pick. Read the policy.
